The story • Slept-on, not small

About Pac

No gimmicks. No handouts. Just poetry, pain, and progress — straight out of Fort Worth.

Honestly I didn’t find music — it found me. My poetry of music is my outlet to closing chapters after chapters. I mean where do I begin, from the beginning or just what you see now. To be honest with you I’m just getting started.
— Fort Worth Pac, from his Spotify bio

Fort Worth Pac — also known as Alkadalic — is a hip-hop artist from Fort Worth, Texas. Self-made, he built his name the hard way: raw, honest records about his highs and lows, with no major label and no cosign — just the city behind him.

His name says it all: P.A.C. stands for Prophecy Alkadalic Control. Every verse is a chapter closing — and a bigger one opening. As he puts it: he’s just getting started. The city already knows. The world is next.
